AIEF Publisher

AIEF Publisher is an internal tool of AIEpicVerse, an independent video studio run by Rehan Faizal. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • Supo flix cinema (@Supoflixcinema)
  • Rehan Faizal (@rehanfaizal6100)
  • AI Epic Verse (@AIEpicVerse-l6h)

The tool uploads videos (by default as private drafts), sets titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and reads basic channel and video information. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users. Only the channel owner signs in, and only to manage their own channels.
