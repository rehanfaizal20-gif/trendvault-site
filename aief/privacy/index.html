Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how AIEF Publisher ("the app"), an internal tool of AIEpicVerse, handles data when the channel owner signs in with a Google account.

Google user data we access

With the owner's permission, the app requests these YouTube API scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – to read the owner's channel details, video list and video status.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl – to manage the owner's own videos, including editing metadata, captions and thumbnails.

How we use it

This data is used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored privately on servers controlled by the owner and are not shared with anyone. Access is limited to the owner's own systems.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with third parties, and no humans read it except the owner managing their own channels.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AIEF Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored tokens stop working, and you can ask us to delete any stored tokens or data by emailing the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
